# Tunisia - energy facts for farmers (official sources, read on 21 November 2026)

## STEG electricity tariffs - tarifs d'électricité STEG, prix du kWh, facture (hors taxes)

Source: STEG, "Les tarifs d'électricité", https://www.steg.com.tn/fr/page/les-tarifs-d%C3%A9lectricit%C3%A9
Tariffs in force from 01/05/2022 (low voltage). They are the latest version published by STEG; check your bill.

Low voltage (basse tension), energy price per monthly consumption band, in millimes per kWh (1000 millimes = 1 dinar):
- Residential up to 50 kWh/month: 62 ; 51-100 kWh: 96 ; 101-200 kWh: 176 ; 201-300 kWh: 218 ; 301-500 kWh: 341 ; above 500 kWh: 414.
- Non-residential up to 100 kWh/month: 104 ; 101-200 kWh: 195 ; 201-300 kWh: 240 ; 301-500 kWh: 333 ; above 500 kWh: 391.
- Add the municipal surtax of 5 millimes/kWh and the FTE tax of 5 millimes/kWh.
- VAT: 7 % on irrigation energy, 19 % on other non-residential uses.
- The low-voltage "Irrigation uniforme" tariff is marked by STEG: "Ce tarif n'est plus accordé" (no longer granted to new customers).

Medium voltage (moyenne tension), from 01/10/2022, source https://www.steg.com.tn/fr/page/tarifs-moyenne-tension :
- Uniform tariff: 291 millimes/kWh (+ power fee 5 000 millimes/kVA/month).
- Time-of-use: day 290, summer morning peak 417, evening peak 377, night 222 millimes/kWh.
- "Pompage pour irrigation" 374 millimes/kWh: marked "no longer granted".
- "Irrigation agricole" tariff exists with load-shedding (effacement) in winter evening peak (September to May).

## Solar production in Tunisia - production solaire, kWh par kWc, ensoleillement

- ANME (https://www.anme.tn/fr/content/solar-photovoltaic): 1 kWp of solar panels produces about 1 450 kWh per year in the north-west and up to 1 830 kWh per year in the extreme south-east.
- PVGIS (European Commission JRC, https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/), our calculation for each governorate capital: from 1 537 kWh/kWp/year (Zaghouan) to 1 834 kWh/kWp/year (Tataouine). Kebili 1 829, Sidi Bouzid 1 707, Kairouan 1 651, Nabeul 1 595, Bizerte 1 588.

## Electricity mix of Tunisia - mix électrique, gaz, importations (Our World in Data, from Ember / Energy Institute)

Source: https://github.com/owid/energy-data
- 2025: demand 24.9 TWh, generation 22.3 TWh, net imports 2.6 TWh.
- About 95 % of Tunisian electricity is produced from natural gas (94.9 % in 2025). Solar was 2.5 % and wind 1.5 % in 2025.
- So every kWh of solar pumping replaces mostly gas-based (and partly imported) electricity.

## Subsidies for solar pumping in agriculture - subvention, prime, aide de l'Etat pour pompe solaire / pompage photovoltaïque agricole (FTE - Fonds de Transition Energétique, ANME)

Source: FTE-ANME, PROSOL-ELEC off-grid ("hors réseau") for agriculture:
https://fte-anme.tn/solutions/agriculture/energies-renouvelables-dans-lagriculture/prosol-elec-hors-reseaux-residentiel/
- Investment subsidy, fixed amount per kWp (kWc) installed:
  - up to 0.25 kWp: 6 000 TND per kWp
  - 0.25 to 0.5 kWp: 4 500 TND per kWp
  - 0.5 to 2 kWp: 3 500 TND per kWp
  - 2 to 5 kWp: 3 000 TND per kWp
  - 5 to 10 kWp: 1 500 TND per kWp
  - above 10 kWp: 1 000 TND per kWp
  - maximum 50 000 TND.
- Energy audit and feasibility study: 70 % of the cost (maximum 30 000 TND each). Support: 70 % (maximum 70 000 TND).
- Process: the approved PV installer submits the request; ANME gives prior approval, the works are done, ANME inspects, then the subsidy is paid.
- Legal texts cited: Décret gouvernemental n° 2016-1123 du 24 août 2016 (self-production of electricity from renewable energy); Décret n° 2017-983 du 26 juillet 2017 (FTE rules), amended by Décret n° 2023-86 du 2 février 2023.
- A grid-connected PROSOL-ELEC programme for agriculture also exists (low and medium voltage):
  https://fte-anme.tn/solutions/agriculture/energies-renouvelables-dans-lagriculture/prosol-elec-connecte-aux-reseaux-residentiel/
- The list of approved PV installers is published by ANME: https://www.anme.tn/
