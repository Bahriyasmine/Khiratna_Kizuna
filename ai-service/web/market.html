📈 Tunisian Market Forecast (2027)

🔻 Part 1 — What will Tunisia lack in 2027? (whole country)

National FAO statistics: the same answer for every governorate. No input needed.

💰 Part 2 — Which crop earns the most for MY water?

Your governorate and planting month change only the water to add (service 1: rain and heat of your region, crop season from the planting month). Example: watermelon planted in April needs 4 044 m³/ha in Nabeul but 6 634 m³/ha in Kebili (hotter, drier). Prices and yields are national (FAOSTAT).

Tunisia lacks it = Tunisia produces less than 90 % of what it needs: a safe market. Market already full = Tunisia already produces 100 % or more: if many farmers plant it, the price can fall.
Dinars per m³ = revenue per hectare ÷ water to add in your governorate. Only crops that can be sown in the open field in the chosen month are ranked (FAO crop calendar of Tunisia); fruit trees are already in the field. See the water month by month in 🌧️ Rainfall & water →

Part 1 — Wheat:
① consumption 2024 = production + imports − exports = 1 150 000 + 1 397 090 − 0 = 2 547 090 t
② self-sufficiency 2024 = 1 150 000 ÷ 2 547 090 = 45 %
③ the Theta time-series model learns production and consumption 2000–2024 and forecasts 2027: production 1 005 150 t, consumption 2 784 270 t → self-sufficiency 36 %, missing 1 779 120 t.
(Tested on past years: about 7–10 % error.)

Part 2 — Watermelon in Nabeul, planted in April:
① farm price 2025 = 624 × 215.7 ÷ 221.0 = 609 TND/t
② yield = average 2022–2024 = 103.2 t/ha → revenue = 103.2 × 609 = 62 826 TND/ha (before costs)
③ water to add (service 1, Nabeul, April) = 4 044 m³/ha
④ dinars per m³ = 62 826 ÷ 4 044 = 15.5 TND per m³ of water
