💬 Khiratna

Hello! I'm Khiratna, your farming assistant. I help you make decisions about farming and energy. How can I help you?

⚙️ How it works (RAG)

1. Knowledge base — 1 300 passages from 5 sourced documents: FAO solar irrigation (2018), IRENA solar pumping (2016), Tunisian energy facts (STEG, FTE/ANME, PVGIS), SARE crop rotation manual (USDA, 2020), crop rotation rules (UC IPM, Clemson).
2. Chunking — PDFs cleaned first (page headers, page numbers and bibliographies removed, broken words re-joined), then whole sentences packed into ~1 000 characters, the last 2 sentences repeated in the next passage; sheets: one passage per section.
3. Embeddings — multilingual-e5-small (local, free, 100 languages), stored in pgvector (PostgreSQL) or local files.
4. Retrieval — the 6 closest passages + the 2 best passages of the sourced sheets.
5. Answer — Gemini 2.5 Flash (thinking off, ~1-2 s) answers only from these passages, in short English sentences; for costs it calls the energy calculator.
6. Voice — 🎤 speech-to-text with Whisper (small, run on our server, English, ~2 s) and 🔊 text-to-speech by the browser, both free.
