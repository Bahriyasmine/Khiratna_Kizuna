⚙️ How it works

  1. The soil map: ISRIC (World Soil Information, an international soil centre) mapped Tunisia's soils for the FAO. Tunisia is cut into 752 zones; for each zone ISRIC gives the main soil and its values in the top 20 cm (pH, salt, lime...).
  2. The value of a governorate: we put a point every 2 km on Tunisia (37 722 points), find the governorate and the soil zone of each point, then take the median (half of the land is above, half below). "Salty area" = the % of points where the soil is salty.
  3. Good or bad? Each value is read with the official classes of the USDA Soil Survey Manual and the FAO Guidelines for Soil Description (e.g. salt: below 2 dS/m = not salty).
Limits: the map is at 1:1 000 000 scale: one zone covers tens of km². It shows the usual soil of an area, not of one field. For one field, a soil lab test is needed.
