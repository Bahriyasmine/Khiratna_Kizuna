⚙️ How it works

  1. The data: the Kaggle "Crop Recommendation Dataset": 2 200 examples (100 for each of 22 crops). Each example gives the soil nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, the temperature, the humidity, the soil pH, the rainfall, and the crop that suits them.
  2. The AI model: a Random Forest = 300 decision trees. Each tree learns rules such as "lots of rain + high humidity → rice". For your values, every tree votes for one crop.
  3. The result: the % next to each crop = the share of the 300 trees that voted for it. The list is sorted.
  4. Accuracy: — tested 5 times on 20 % of the data the model had not seen (5-fold cross-validation).
